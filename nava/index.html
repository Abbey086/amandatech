Questions to ask every school

  • What are the total fees per term, including extras?
  • How do pupils get to and from school?
  • What was last year's UNEB performance?
  • How big are the classes?
  • What is lunch and what does it cost?
  • How do teachers report to parents?

About

NAVA Agency Uganda was founded in 2026 in Kololo by Muhammad Salaam, a Ugandan marketing professional. We saw two problems: parents walking tired looking for schools, and good schools with empty chairs. So we built a bridge.

We are not a big Kololo agency charging 3M. We are your neighbors in different regions, and we understand parents because we live where you are.

Mission: Every child learns in a good school, and every good school is full.
